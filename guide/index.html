1 Signing in

Open the app link on your phone or PC and log in.

Open the app

Use the link your manager sent. On the counter iPad it's already set up.

Enter your ORMS email & password

Use your own @orms.co.za login. Don't have one yet? Ask Arno or Bronwyn to set you up.

💡 Tip: On your phone you can "Add to Home Screen" so it opens like an app.

2 The main screen

When you log in you'll see the pulse of your branch.

  • Summary tiles at the top — Pending · Active · Urgent · Stuck counts. Tap one to jump straight to it.
  • Three tabs: Pending (booked / in transit), Active (in the workshop or back at the branch waiting), Collected (done).
  • Search box — find any repair by job number, client name, phone number or notes.
  • Branch filters — show only Cape Town, Bellville or Somerset.

3 Logging a repair

When a client drops off an item for repair.

Pick the branch it's going to

Cape Town does the repairs, so that's usually the choice.

Fill in the details

Client name, contact number, the job number (it auto-capitalises), and the item.

Add anything useful (optional)

Tick 🔴 URGENT for rush jobs, add Notes like "needed by Friday", or snap a condition photo of the item.

Buy-in / used gear?

Tick the Buy-in box and add the stock code + used-gear doc number. It fills the name in as "Orms Inhouse Repair".

Tap "Log Repair"

Done! It appears at the top of the list. If that job number is already open, you'll get a gentle warning.

4 Moving it along — the "Update" button

Every repair has an Update ▸ button. Tap it to see its journey and do the next step.

A progress bar at the top shows exactly where the repair is. The steps are:

Received by

When it arrives at Cape Town — enter who received it.

Repair Done

When the workshop has finished.

Send to Branch / Collect here

Pick which branch the client will collect from — or collect / courier straight from Cape Town.

Received by

When it arrives back at the collection branch — enter who received it.

Collect

When the client picks it up (see step 6).

The app only closes the Update screen when you tap Close — so you can type freely without it disappearing.

5 Button colours — easy to remember

The colour tells you what a button does.

GreenMove it to the next step (Received, Repair Done, Send to Branch).
BlueCollect / hand it to the client — the final step.
RedUrgent, or delete. Deleting needs a manager PIN.

And each row has a coloured strip down its left edge showing its stage at a glance:

Amber — in transit
Blue — in the workshop
Green — ready to collect
Red — urgent

6 Collecting a repair

When the client comes to pick up their item.

Find the repair

Search the job number or client name, or use the counter iPad's collect view.

Tap the blue Collect button

✔ Collect (client is here)

Enter who's collecting & the branch

The person's name and which branch they're collecting at.

Client signs on screen

They sign with a finger (or mouse) to confirm — it's stamped with the date and time.

Collecting somewhere else or couriering? Those options are right there in the collect screen.

7 Finding repairs & flags

  • Search matches job number, client name, phone, notes and stock code.
  • 🔴 URGENT repairs sort to the top of the list.
  • ⚠ Stuck shows on repairs sitting too long. Use the "⚠ Stuck only" filter to see just those.
  • 📝 Notes appear right on the row so you never miss "needed by Friday".
  • Lists show 10 at a time with Prev / Next paging.

8 Made a mistake? No problem

Tapped the wrong step? It's easy to fix.

  • Open the repair's Update ▸ and tap "↩ Undo last step" — it walks the repair back one stage (even an accidental collection).
  • Need to change a note or urgency? Open Update ▸ and edit them there any time.
  • Stuck on anything? Tap the "? Help" button in the app for a quick refresher.
✅ That's it! Log it → move it along with the green buttons → collect with the blue button. You've got this.
